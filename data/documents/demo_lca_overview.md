# Demonstration note: configuring a life-cycle assessment

This file is a synthetic demonstration document written for the LCA Knowledge Assistant MVP. It is not a standard, scientific publication, or authoritative guidance.

A life-cycle assessment (LCA), also referred to in French as analyse du cycle de vie (ACV), studies potential environmental impacts associated with a product system. The scope and system boundary should be defined before selecting inventory data and an impact assessment method.

A cradle-to-gate study boundary considers processes from raw material extraction and upstream supply activities through the factory gate. A cradle-to-grave boundary extends the study to later life-cycle stages, potentially including use and end-of-life processes. The appropriate boundary depends on the study goal and scope.

An LCA workflow may involve software for modelling product systems, databases for life-cycle inventory data, and impact assessment methods for evaluating environmental impact categories. Examples of impact categories include climate change and water use.

The selection of a resource should be checked against the study's geography, system boundary, product sector, data quality needs, impact categories, and methodological requirements. A text similarity score alone is not proof that a resource is technically suitable.
